READING THE RESULT
  * A hot region means: many child alleles absent from both listed parents within ~60 informative SNVs, more than that trio's own background predicts.
  * Tags are possible mundane explanations. A region carrying none of them is unexplained by THESE checks only; it is not evidence of any cause.
  * Mendelian errors are rarer in the phased panel than in raw calls (phasing can use the pedigree). Hot regions that exist in raw genotypes but not here would be hidden by this choice.

  * The focus coordinates and the preprint's exact signal definition. * Family IDs (none bundled). * Cell-line (EBV) artifacts need read-level data (see personal pipeline).
